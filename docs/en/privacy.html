Privacy Policy

One Tap Timer — September 9, 2026

One Tap Timer does not connect to the internet to run timers. There are no accounts. Nothing about you leaves your device, and the developer cannot see it. The only exception is the optional tip ("A cup of coffee"), which talks to the App Store. Apple handles the payment; the developer never receives your card details.

Information we collect

None. No sign-up, no email address. No analytics, no advertising measurement. The only in-app purchase is an optional tip ("A cup of coffee"); every feature works without it. Apple (the App Store) processes the purchase, and the developer receives only the proceeds.

Notifications

To alert you while you are not looking at the screen, the app schedules a local notification for the end time. Only the time and the preset length are stored. No external server is involved (this is not push notification). If you decline, the timer still runs but cannot alert you while the screen is away.

Stored on your device

Only your preset time, the color you picked, and the end time of the running timer, so the app can start with the same time next time and show the seconds on the watch-face complication. This never leaves the device and is deleted with the app.

Sharing with third parties

None. There is nobody to share with.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated.
