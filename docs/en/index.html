One Tap Timer

A timer for Apple Watch and iPhone that starts the moment you open it — September 9, 2026

Open the app and your preset time (1 minute 30 seconds by default) starts right away. When it ends, the watch taps you once and stops. It never keeps buzzing, so you can leave it alone. Put it on your watch face as a complication and it is truly one tap.

How it works

  1. Open the app (or tap the complication). The timer starts.
  2. The water level drains across the whole screen. Under one minute, only the seconds are shown, large.
  3. At the end you get a single haptic, and the screen turns white with “DONE”.
  4. Tap the screen to start over.

Changing the time

Tap the gear in the top left. Use + − (hold to keep going), the preset chips, or the Digital Crown, then tap “Start with this”. The app remembers it for next time. Anything from 10 seconds to 60 minutes.

FAQ

Will it notify me when my wrist is down?

Yes. A single notification arrives at the end time and taps your wrist. That is why the app asks for notification permission once. Without it the timer still runs, but it cannot alert you while you are not looking at the screen.

How do I stop it?

There is no stop button. Left alone, it just taps once at the end. To start over, tap the screen.

If I open it again after it ended, does it restart?

Within 30 seconds of the end, you see the “DONE” screen (so a glance at your wrist does not start the next one). After that, opening the app starts a fresh timer. If it is still running, you get the running timer.

Opening it from the watch face

Long-press the watch face → Edit → pick a complication slot → choose “One Tap Timer”. The complication shows your preset in seconds (for example 90) and switches to the remaining time while running. If it does not appear in the list, restart the Apple Watch once.

The haptic feels weak

Check Settings → Sounds & Haptics → Haptic Strength on the watch. Turning on “Prominent Haptic” helps too.

Does it make a sound?

No. Haptics only. The end-time notification follows your watch’s notification settings.

Apple Watch only?

Yes. You can install it on the Apple Watch without installing the iPhone app.

iPhone only?

Yes. The same timer runs on iPhone. Settings are kept separately on each device.
